SmallHold Privacy Policy

The landlord toolkit for small portfolios.

Effective date: March 22, 2026

The short version: SmallHold stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect personal information, do not have servers, and do not sell or share any data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

SmallHold is a fully offline app. We collect no data whatsoever.

All information you enter into SmallHold — properties, units, tenants, transactions, and receipt images — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Passwords or account credentials (no accounts exist)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • Cookies or browser fingerprints
  • Financial or banking information

3. Device Permissions

SmallHold may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera: Used solely to photograph receipts for expense tracking. Images are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: Used to select existing photos of receipts. Selected images remain on your device.

These permissions are optional. The app functions fully without granting them.

4. On-Device Processing

SmallHold uses Apple's Vision framework to perform optical character recognition (OCR) on scanned receipts. This processing happens entirely on your device — no images or extracted text are sent to external servers.

5. In-App Purchases

SmallHold offers optional subscriptions managed entirely through the Apple App Store. We do not process payments directly and have no access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. We have no servers and no mechanism to access your data.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Deleting the app removes all associated data. You can also delete individual properties, tenants, and transactions within the app at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

SmallHold is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal information is collected from any user, this concern is inherently mitigated.

9. Third-Party Services

SmallHold uses only Apple's native frameworks (SwiftData, StoreKit, Vision, VisionKit, PDFKit). No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are included in the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
