PRIVACY POLICY

SmallHold — Property tracking made simple
Effective date: March 22, 2026

Judge Dean LLC ("we", "our", "us") built SmallHold as a freemium iOS application for landlords to track rental properties, tenants, expenses, and generate Schedule E reports. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

None. SmallHold does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data you enter into the app stays exclusively on your device.

Data Storage

SmallHold stores your property, tenant, transaction, and receipt data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

Device Permissions

SmallHold may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera: Used solely to photograph receipts for expense records. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: Used to select existing receipt images from your library. Selected images are stored locally within the app.

You may deny these permissions and still use all other features of the app.

Third-Party Services

SmallHold does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics or crash reporting tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage or sync services
  • User tracking or fingerprinting technologies

In-App Purchases

SmallHold offers an optional "SmallHold Pro" upgrade through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no data at all, no special provisions are necessary.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
