CampShhh Privacy Policy

Find your quiet. Rate the noise.

Effective date: March 3, 2026

The short version: CampShhh does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

CampShhh is designed with privacy at its core. We collect the minimum amount of data needed to provide the service:

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A randomly generated identifier stored locally on your device. This is used solely to prevent duplicate ratings and is never linked to your identity.
  • Location Data: With your permission, CampShhh accesses your device location to show nearby campgrounds and sort results by distance. Your location is processed on-device and is never stored on our servers.
  • Noise Ratings: When you submit a noise rating for a campground, the rating value and your anonymous device identifier are stored to calculate aggregate noise scores.
  • Comments: If you leave a field report (comment) on a campground, the text content is stored alongside your anonymous device identifier. Do not include personal information in comments.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Passwords or account credentials (no accounts exist)
  • Precise GPS location history
  • Photos, contacts, or other device data
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • Cookies or browser fingerprints

3. How We Use Information

The limited data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Display crowdsourced noise ratings for campgrounds
  • Prevent duplicate or fraudulent ratings from a single device
  • Show campgrounds sorted by proximity to your current location
  • Display user-submitted field reports (comments)

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. Period.

Our backend infrastructure is hosted on Supabase (cloud database provider). Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS and stored in accordance with Supabase's security practices.

5. Data Retention

Noise ratings and comments are retained indefinitely to maintain the accuracy of aggregate campground scores. Since no personal information is collected, there is no personal data to delete.

If you wish to have specific comments removed, please contact us at the email address below with the campground name and approximate date of the comment.

6. Location Permissions

CampShhh requests location access ("While Using the App") to sort campgrounds by distance and center the map on your position. This permission is optional — the app functions without it, defaulting to a US-wide view. Your location is never transmitted to our servers or stored.

7. Children's Privacy

CampShhh is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no personal information is collected from any user, this concern is inherently mitigated.

8. Third-Party Services

CampShhh uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Backend database and API hosting. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Maps: Map rendering within the app. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap: Campground location data. OSM Privacy Policy

9. Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Our database access is secured with row-level security policies and API key authentication.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
