Privacy policy · Updated September 30, 2026

CampShhh helps you find US campgrounds and prepare for booking. Browsing requires no email or password. We do not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or share data for targeted advertising.

Version 2.0 stores Saved places and booking plans on your device. Community submissions and follow updates are currently restricted while their security protections are reviewed. This policy also explains records created by earlier releases.

On your device

Saved campground identifiers, booking plans, travel dates, notes, reminder settings and app preferences are stored locally. Device backups may include this data. Removing a plan in CampShhh does not remove a separate calendar copy. Calendar copies follow the privacy and synchronization settings of the calendar you choose.

Location, searches and maps

Location permission is optional and helps show nearby places. Version 2.0 filters its bundled campground catalog on your device. Earlier releases could send search terms and nearby coordinates to our database service. Apple Maps receives requests for areas you view; place searches may use Apple’s geocoder. External map services apply their own policies. We do not provide a background location-history feature.

Public information and earlier contributions

Selected public campground experiences are summarized in a bundled research ledger with source links and check dates. Research estimates are editorial interpretations, not review-platform star ratings or sound measurements. The app does not fetch those review websites until you open a source link.

Earlier app releases collected submitted noise ratings, stay months, field reports, helpful votes, campground follows, moderation reports and related timestamps. A pseudonymous identifier derived from a device identifier linked these records. Public reports and ratings can be visible to other people; pseudonymous does not mean no data was collected. Some historical records have unverified provenance and are withheld from the current research scores.

Historical server records remain subject to support and moderation review. Community submissions are currently restricted in version 2.0; no new contribution account is required for browsing. Contact support to request review or removal of an earlier submission. We may need independent evidence of ownership; never send passwords, authentication tokens or private recovery codes.

Service requests and support

Supabase hosts public campground and historical report services. Network requests disclose technical information such as an IP address to the receiving service, which may retain operational logs and backups. We do not use these requests for advertising. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to handle your request.

Reminders and optional tips

Personal booking reminders are scheduled on your device with permission. Delivery depends on iOS settings. Earlier campground-update features could store follow preferences, inbox entries and push notification tokens on our backend.

Apple processes optional consumable tips. We do not receive payment-card numbers. The app uses purchase information supplied by Apple to finish the transaction and show its result. Tips unlock no features. Apple manages its purchase history and refund process.

Retention, choices and security

Local information remains until you remove it or clear the app’s local data; backup and calendar copies are managed separately. Clearing or reinstalling the app does not delete earlier server submissions. Server records, support correspondence, administrative records and provider backups may remain where needed to operate the service, address requests or meet legal obligations. We do not promise a deletion or backup-expiry schedule that has not been verified.

Manage location, notification and calendar permissions in iOS Settings. You can browse without location access, choose not to export a calendar event, and decline reminders. Requests to our hosted service use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Other services and children

Apple Maps and purchases follow Apple’s privacy policy. Hosted database requests follow Supabase’s privacy policy. Campground map data is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors; external OSM services follow the OSM Foundation privacy policy. Reservation operators and research websites have their own policies when you open them.

CampShhh is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. If our enabled features or data practices change, we will update this policy and the app’s disclosures.
