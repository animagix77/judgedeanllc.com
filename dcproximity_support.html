How It Works

DCProximity is a data center intelligence platform for real estate investors and community stakeholders. Browse an interactive map of US data centers, monitor construction alerts, read community reviews, and analyze property value impacts — all from your iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to create an account?
    No. All core features — the map, alerts, reviews, and watchlist — work without an account. An account is only needed for Pro features like investment reports and personalized alerts.
  • What is the Pro subscription?
    DCProximity Pro unlocks advanced investment analysis, AI-powered reports, and custom alert configurations. It is available as a monthly subscription through the App Store.
  • How is property data sourced?
    Property value data is aggregated from public sources and real estate data providers. Values shown are estimates intended for informational purposes and should not be used as the sole basis for investment decisions.
  • Where do community reviews come from?
    Reviews are aggregated from multiple public platforms including Reddit, Google, YouTube, Yelp, and Facebook. Sentiment analysis is applied to categorize reviews as positive, mixed, or negative.
  • How do construction alerts work?
    Alerts are generated when new data center construction, expansion, or maintenance activity is detected. Each alert includes an impact level (critical, high, or medium) and details about the affected area.
  • The app is not loading or showing errors.
    Make sure you have an internet connection and try restarting the app. If the problem persists, please contact us using the email below with a description of the issue.
