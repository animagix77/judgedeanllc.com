How It Works

SmallHold helps landlords with small property portfolios track their rentals, tenants, income, and expenses — all from their iPhone. No account needed, no cloud required. Everything stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to create an account?
    No. SmallHold works entirely on your device with no sign-up or login required.
  • How do I add a property?
    Tap the + button on the Dashboard or use the Quick Add feature to add a property with its address, type, and units.
  • How does receipt scanning work?
    When adding an expense, tap the camera icon to photograph a receipt. SmallHold uses on-device OCR to automatically extract the vendor name, amount, and date.
  • What is Schedule E?
    Schedule E is the IRS form used to report rental income and expenses. SmallHold generates a summary matching the Schedule E categories, which you can export as PDF or CSV for your accountant.
  • What's included in the free version?
    The free tier lets you manage 1 property with unlimited units and transactions. Upgrade to Pro to manage unlimited properties, export PDF/CSV reports, and attach receipt photos.
  • How do I manage my subscription?
    Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your iPhone to change or cancel your plan.
  • Is my data backed up?
    SmallHold data is stored locally and is included in your standard iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or Finder/iTunes backup). We recommend keeping backups enabled.
  • The app is not working correctly.
    Try force-quitting and restarting the app. If the problem persists, make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store. If issues continue, contact us below.
