How It Works

CampShhh helps campers find peaceful campgrounds by crowdsourcing noise level ratings. No account required — just open the app, browse campgrounds near you, and see what fellow campers have reported.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to create an account?
    No. CampShhh is completely anonymous. No login, no sign-up, no personal data collected.
  • How are noise levels calculated?
    Noise ratings are crowdsourced from campers who have visited each campground. Ratings range from 1 (silent) to 5 (very loud) and are averaged across all submissions.
  • Can I rate a campground?
    Yes! Tap any campground to open its detail page, then use the noise slider to submit your rating. You can also leave a written field report for other campers.
  • Why is my campground not listed?
    Our database covers thousands of US campgrounds sourced from OpenStreetMap. If a campground is missing, please let us know via the contact email below and we will work to add it.
  • What does the map timeline do?
    The timeline scrubber lets you see how noise levels change by month and season, so you can plan your trip for the quietest time of year.
  • The app is not loading or showing errors.
    Try pulling down to refresh the campground list. If the problem persists, make sure you have an internet connection and try restarting the app. If issues continue, contact us below.
