A little help finding quiet.

Support · Updated September 30, 2026

CampShhh brings US campground discovery, selected public-source noise research and booking preparation together. Version 2.0 adds the daylight Trail Atlas design, booking guidance, timing calculations and personal plans.

We do not make reservations, monitor live inventory or guarantee a quiet stay.

What does a noise estimate mean?

The 1–5 scale runs from quieter to louder. Version 2.0 interprets dated, firsthand public reports where enough independent evidence is available. An estimate includes sources, dates and limitations; it is not a decibel measurement or a platform’s star rating. Small samples and changing conditions limit what it can predict.

Research coverage is incomplete. “Unknown” or “Research pending” means there is not enough supported evidence, not that a campground is quiet. We do not turn unverified historical app scores into current research estimates.

Can I submit a rating or report?

Community submissions and follow updates are currently restricted in version 2.0 during a security review. You can still browse, save places and make personal booking plans without an account. Earlier releases may show historical reports; their provenance is not always verified.

How do booking dates work?

Open the Booking field guide and check the linked official operator. Where a researched rule supports it, the calculator estimates a reservation opening date. An exact release time is shown only where its time and time zone are supported. Minimum stays, arrival-day restrictions, loops and seasonal exceptions can change the result.

Availability is checked on the operator’s site. Community tips describe experiences and are not official rules. Check the source and its review date before acting, and keep alternative dates or sites ready.

Do my plans and reminders sync?

Saved places and personal plans live on this device. Reminder delivery depends on notification permission and iOS settings. A calendar export creates a separate copy after you save in the system calendar editor. Editing or deleting a CampShhh plan does not change that calendar copy.

Why is a campground missing or incorrect?

Campground locations come from a US-focused catalog that includes OpenStreetMap data. It is not a complete inventory, and named places or boundaries can be wrong. Send us the campground’s name, state and official link so we can review it. Unknown booking rules or noise scores are shown honestly.

What if search or the keyboard gets stuck?

Use the keyboard’s Search or Done control to dismiss it, or return to Explore. Version 2.0 keeps focused fields and important actions within the scrollable area. If a button is still covered, send your iPhone model, app version, text-size setting and a redacted screenshot.

The bundled discovery catalog can be browsed without a connection. Maps, linked websites, purchase services and historical report requests may need internet access. For a persistent loading problem, close and reopen the app and contact support with the steps that reproduce it.

How do tips work?

Kindling, Campfire and Bonfire are optional consumable purchases processed by Apple. They support development and unlock no features. Only confirm a purchase if you intend to pay. For a charge or refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem service.

How do I remove an earlier report?

Contact us with the campground and approximate date. Clearing app data does not remove a server submission. We may need independent proof of ownership to avoid removing someone else’s report. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, receipts containing private account details or recovery codes.
