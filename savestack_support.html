How SaveStack Works

SaveStack helps New Jersey homeowners discover state and utility rebates, tax credits, and incentive programs for home energy upgrades. Answer a few quick questions about your home, and we match you with every program you qualify for.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the free version include?

The free version shows you how many incentive programs you qualify for and your estimated total savings. You can see the name and category of each qualifying program at no cost.

What does the premium report unlock?

The premium report ($6.99, one-time purchase) unlocks full details for each incentive: exact savings amounts, direct application links, required documentation, deadlines, and which programs can be stacked together for maximum savings.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. SaveStack is a local-first app. All your quiz answers and results stay on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What states do you cover?

SaveStack currently covers New Jersey only, including state programs (NJ Clean Energy, Charge Up NJ, Comfort Partners) and utility rebates (PSE&G, JCP&L, Atlantic City Electric).

Are the incentive amounts accurate?

We regularly verify our data against official program sources. Amounts shown are estimates based on typical eligibility. Actual amounts may vary based on your specific situation. Always confirm with the program administrator before applying.

Can renters use this app?

Yes! While most rebates are for homeowners, some programs (like utility appliance rebates and EV incentives) are available to renters as well. SaveStack filters results based on whether you own or rent.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Your premium purchase is tied to your Apple ID. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, you can restore your purchase through the App Store at no additional cost.

How do I request a refund?

Since purchases are processed through the App Store, refund requests should be submitted through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support directly.
