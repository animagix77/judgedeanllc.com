DCProximity Privacy Policy

Data Center Investment Intel

Effective date: March 28, 2026

The short version: DCProximity does not require an account to use core features, collects minimal data, and does not sell or share any personal information with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

DCProximity is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum data needed to provide the service:

  • Account Information (optional): If you create an account to access Pro features, we collect your email address and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google OAuth, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Subscription Data: Pro subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive a confirmation of your subscription status but do not store payment card details.
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data such as which screens are viewed and general feature usage patterns. This data cannot be linked to individual users.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise GPS location or location history
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Financial account numbers or investment portfolio data
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Browsing history or cookies

3. How We Use Information

The limited data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide access to data center intelligence features (map, alerts, reviews, analytics)
  • Manage Pro subscription status and feature access
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to support inquiries

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. Period.

Our backend services use industry-standard cloud infrastructure. Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact us at the email address below.

Anonymous usage analytics are retained in aggregate form and cannot be attributed to individual users.

6. Children's Privacy

DCProximity is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Third-Party Services

DCProximity uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store: Subscription and payment processing. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Google OAuth (optional): Sign-in authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy

8. Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. User passwords are salted and hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We implement access controls and regularly review our security practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
