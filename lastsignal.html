LAST SIGNAL

The Journey

About

Last Signal - The Journey is an interactive narrative thriller for iOS. You play as Dean, a father on the run, protecting Eli — his son whose consciousness lives inside a titanium AI chassis. Every choice you make affects your survival. There are no retries. Only consequences.

Privacy Policy

Last Signal - The Journey does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely offline on your device.

Data collection: None. We do not collect analytics, usage data, or personal information of any kind.

Third-party services: None. The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Game saves: Your game progress is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions: The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device features.
