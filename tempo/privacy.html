Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Tempo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Tempo mobile application.

Information We Collect

Tempo collects the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Profile information: Your name, AFib type, diagnosis date, known triggers, and medications. This information is stored locally on your device.
  • Health tracking data: Episode logs, journal entries, mood check-ins, and experiment data that you enter into the app. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Chat messages: When you use the Talk to Tempo chat feature, your messages are processed through Crisp, our third-party chat platform. Crisp may store conversation data on their servers. See Crisp's Privacy Policy for details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the Tempo app experience
  • To display your profile context during chat conversations so our support team can provide relevant guidance
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns (anonymized and aggregated only)

Data Storage

Your health data (episodes, journal entries, experiments, mood check-ins) is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to external servers unless you explicitly use the chat feature, in which case limited profile context may be shared with the chat session to provide better support.

Third-Party Services

Tempo uses the following third-party services:

  • Crisp: Live chat platform for the Talk to Tempo feature. Crisp Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: For typography rendering. Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your health data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other commercial entities.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored in the app (visible directly within the app)
  • Delete your data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app
  • Request deletion of any chat conversation data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Tempo is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical Disclaimer

Tempo is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult your cardiologist or healthcare provider for medical decisions. This app is designed to help you track lifestyle patterns and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
