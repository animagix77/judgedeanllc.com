Frequently Asked Questions

What can I do with Tempo?

Record episodes, make daily mood and symptom check-ins, keep journal notes, and review your History and Insights. The updated app also offers weekly recaps, appointment questions, and PDF reports. Records are self-reported; patterns do not establish a cause or replace medical care.

Do I need an account?

Local tracking works without an account. Cloud backup uses an optional email/password account. Companion uses service authentication and asks permission before sharing conversation and profile context. Account and online features may be unavailable in some versions or while services are unavailable.

Where is my data stored?

Records and conversation history save on your device. Cloud backup uploads only when you select Back up now in Settings > Account & backup. Backups include your profile, episodes, journals/check-ins, and experiments, and exclude chat history and appointment questions. Before retirement, Companion sent the conversation and approved profile context through the Tempo service to Anthropic. The Tempo AI service is now disabled. See our Privacy Policy for storage, retention, and provider details.

Who replies in Companion?

The Tempo Companion service is discontinued. Earlier replies were generated by AI and were not medical care, live support, or emergency monitoring. Discontinuing the service does not automatically delete conversation history on your device or copies previously processed by a provider.

Is Tempo free?

Local tracking in installed versions remains free. The AI Companion service has been discontinued. Manage any existing Tempo Pro subscription through your Apple Account, and contact support if you need help with a subscription question.

How do I restore or cancel Tempo Pro?

Use Restore purchases on the Tempo Pro purchase screen while signed into the Apple account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel your subscription in your device's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting Tempo data or a cloud account does not cancel your subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records in History, or use Settings > Delete local data to remove this device's records, chat history, and preferences. For cloud data, use Account & backup to delete your backup or cloud account separately. Deleting a cloud account also deletes its saved backup and keeps local records. For service-held information or conversations from earlier versions, email support. Local deletion does not automatically erase provider-held copies.

How do reminders and quiet hours work?

In Settings > Gentle reminders, turn on the optional daily reminder, choose your time and quiet hours, then save. Notification permission is requested when you enable reminders. If your chosen time falls within quiet hours, the reminder moves to the end of that window. Reminders contain no health record details. If reminders are paused, check notification permission in your device settings.

Why is an online feature unavailable?

AI Companion is unavailable because the Tempo AI service has been discontinued. Local tracking remains available in installed versions. For problems accessing your existing records or account and backup controls, email support with the exact message shown in the app.

What if my version still has the earlier chat?

Earlier versions may provide chat through Crisp. The updated app uses AI Companion instead. Consult the Earlier versions section of our Privacy Policy, and email support for questions or deletion requests about an earlier conversation.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email judgedeanllc@gmail.com with your device model, iOS and app versions, the steps that caused the problem, and any error message. If you include a screenshot, hide names, health records, and other sensitive information.
