PRIVACY POLICY

Last Signal - The Journey
Effective date: March 1, 2026

Judge Dean LLC ("we", "our", "us") built Last Signal - The Journey as a premium iOS application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

None. Last Signal - The Journey does not collect any personal information whatsoever. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

Data Storage

The only data the app stores is your game progress (saved game state), which is kept locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

Third-Party Services

Last Signal - The Journey does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics or crash reporting tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage or sync services
  • User tracking or fingerprinting technologies

Device Permissions

The app does not request access to any device features such as:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Location services
  • Photo library
  • Health data
  • Notifications

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no data at all, no special provisions are necessary. The app's content is rated for general audiences, though it contains narrative themes of survival and tension.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
